Crownpaw · 魔王的貓 · Privacy / 私隱政策

Internal beta · 內部測試版

Updated / 更新：2026-11-22

Game progress / 遊戲進度

Progress, settings, inventory and purchase transaction identifiers are stored on your device using browser storage or native Preferences. No game account or cloud save is currently provided. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove progress. 本遊戲在裝置儲存進度、設定、裝備及購買交易識別碼，目前沒有遊戲帳戶或雲端存檔。清除資料或移除程式可能令進度遺失。

Purchases / 購買

On iOS, Apple StoreKit processes purchases and verifies transactions. On Android, a configured Google Play and RevenueCat storefront processes purchase records and entitlement status. We do not receive your card number. Consumable currency that has been spent cannot be restored by the local game. 設定完成後，Apple／Google 及 RevenueCat 會處理購買、收據、使用者識別碼及權益狀態，以驗證及恢復合資格購買。我們不會收到信用卡號碼；已使用的消耗貨幣不會由本機遊戲恢復。

Optional advertising / 自選廣告

Rewarded ads are optional. When enabled, Google AdMob and its configured partners may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions and diagnostics. Optional ads are requested as non-personalized ads. Applicable consent choices are provided by Google. Declining an ad does not stop normal gameplay. You can revisit ad privacy choices in Settings. 獎勵廣告由玩家選擇觀看。啟用後，Google AdMob 及其設定的合作夥伴可能處理裝置識別碼、由 IP 推算的大概位置、廣告互動及診斷資料；適用時會先徵求同意及追蹤許可。拒絕觀看不影響正常遊玩，可在設定重新管理廣告私隱選項。

Your choices / 你的選擇

You may disable sound, music and haptics, decline optional ads, and clear local data through your browser/device. Third-party purchase records follow the provider's retention policy. 可關閉音效、音樂及震動，拒絕自選廣告，或透過瀏覽器／裝置清除本機資料。第三方交易記錄按供應商政策保留。
