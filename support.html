購買後未收到道具

先連線並重新開啟遊戲，讓商店補發已確認嘅交易。新手禮包同當季通行證可以喺「設定 → 恢復購買」還原。寶石餘額同進度儲存喺裝置，未查清問題前請保留遊戲資料。

廣告獎勵

睇完獎勵廣告，關閉廣告返回遊戲後即可領取。如果未收到，請提供當時揀嘅獎勵同時間。

回報問題

請寫低遊戲版本、裝置型號、發生時間同操作步驟。購買查詢可附訂單編號。請勿附上密碼、驗證碼或付款卡資料。

Missing a purchase?

Reopen the game while connected so it can deliver confirmed purchases. In Settings, choose Restore purchases for the starter pack and the current season pass. Gems and progress are saved on your device. Keep the app installed while we investigate.

Missing an ad reward?

Finish the rewarded ad and close it to return to the game. If the reward is missing, include the reward you chose and the time.

Report a problem

Include the game version, device, approximate time and steps. For a purchase, you may include your order ID. Keep passwords, verification codes and card details private.
